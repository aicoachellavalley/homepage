# Mastercard Forecasts 300 Million Agent-Assisted Shoppers by 2030; Teens Twice as Willing as Parents

**Date:** September 10, 2026

## Signal

Mastercard published "A Short History of the Future of Shopping and Payments" on September 8, 2026. Its headline forecast is that more than 300 million online shoppers — roughly one in ten — could routinely use AI agents to shop and pay by 2030. The forecast was developed with four futurists across the U.S., Europe and Asia and is presented as a projection.

The report's survey component is the measured finding. Mastercard and Opinium surveyed 26,000 parents and teenagers across 13 countries in June and July 2026. Asked about a fully automated assistant that recommends products, chooses among available options and completes the purchase under rules the user sets, 27 percent of teenagers said they would likely use it, against 16 percent of parents. The report advises retailers that product information, claims and policies should become machine-readable so agents can find and verify them, and names trust, identity, authorization and transparency as the infrastructure agentic commerce depends on.

## Context

Two facts in the report bear directly on Coachella Valley operators. The first is the age gradient: the cohort most willing to delegate purchasing to an agent is the cohort that will be adults with discretionary income within the forecast window. The second is the stated merchant requirement — machine-readable product information, claims and policies — which is the same requirement AICV's Agent Preview census has been measuring across the valley's merchant categories since June 2026, and which most measured merchants do not yet meet.

Together with Meta's Muse launch and Stripe's single-use card for agents in the same week, the report describes a chain that is now assembling end to end: consumer intent held by an agent, evaluation against machine-readable merchant data, and payment without merchant integration. Mastercard's contribution is the demand-side estimate and the plain statement of what merchants must publish to be evaluated at all.

The 300 million figure is a forecast, not a measurement. Mastercard's newsroom refuses automated retrieval; the figures above were confirmed through independent coverage of the same release. AICV News, the nonprofit's publication, relayed this report for a general audience: https://aicv.news/field-notes/mastercard-agentic-commerce-report
