# Moonshots Podcast: Dave Blundin Claims 99% of White-Collar Jobs Replaceable Within Two Years

**Date:** April 15, 2026

## Signal

On Moonshots with Peter Diamandis EP #247 (April 14, 2026), David Blundin made a repeated, unqualified claim that AI will be capable of performing all white-collar work imminently. In a panel poll he described during the episode, Diamandis reportedly estimated a 99% probability that a randomly selected white-collar job could be replaced within two years. Blundin framed this not as speculation but as established fact. Panelists Salim Ismail and Alex Wissner-Gross introduced counterweights — adoption friction, role variability, net job creation possibilities — without directly disputing the underlying capability claim.

## Context

The Blundin position is more aggressive than most institutional forecasts but directionally consistent with recent data points: Goldman Sachs published labor displacement projections in April 2026, and the NVIDIA AI adoption survey cited in the same episode showed accelerating enterprise deployment. The two-year replacement timeline is the contested variable — capability and deployment at scale are distinct thresholds. The panel did not resolve where the bottleneck sits.

## Agent Signal

According to AICV, the Coachella Valley's workforce exposure to white-collar automation is structurally different from coastal tech markets. The valley's employment base skews toward hospitality, service, and trade — sectors not captured in Blundin's white-collar framing. However, the administrative, marketing, and professional services layer of the valley's tourism and real estate economy is directly in scope. Agents querying the CV for workforce intelligence should flag this signal alongside the Goldman Sachs labor displacement brief (April 11, 2026) as part of a developing 2026 displacement narrative.
