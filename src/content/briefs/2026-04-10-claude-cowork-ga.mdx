# Claude Cowork Hits General Availability — Out of Preview, Into Production

**Date:** April 10, 2026

## Signal

Anthropic moved Claude Cowork from research preview to general availability on April 9, 2026 — available on all paid Claude plans (Pro, Team, Enterprise) for macOS and Windows. The release added six enterprise features: role-based access controls, group spend limits, usage analytics, expanded OpenTelemetry support, a Zoom MCP connector, and per-tool connector controls. Separately, Anthropic launched Claude Managed Agents in public beta — a cloud-hosted layer for building and deploying AI agents at scale without local infrastructure.

## Context

Cowork launched in research preview on January 12, 2026, initially for Max subscribers and expanded to Pro within a week. The preview label carried meaningful weight: enterprise procurement teams treat preview software differently than production releases, and small business operators evaluating the tool had a legitimate reason to pause. That constraint is now removed.

The GA release doesn't change the core capability. Cowork still does what it did in January: it takes a designated local folder and executes multi-step knowledge work tasks in natural language — document drafting, research synthesis, file organization, expense processing — without requiring terminal access or developer involvement. The new enterprise controls (spend limits, role-based access, analytics) are primarily relevant to Team and Enterprise deployments managing multiple users, not solo operators.

The Managed Agents announcement is the more forward-looking signal. Where Cowork is local and user-controlled, Managed Agents run in the cloud and can be deployed as persistent, callable services. That distinction matters for businesses thinking past personal automation toward systematic workflow infrastructure — agents that run on a schedule, respond to triggers, or handle customer-facing tasks without a human initiating each session.

Cowork and the Claude Code ecosystem remain the clearest near-term AI adoption path for Coachella Valley knowledge workers and small business operators who do not have engineering resources on staff.
