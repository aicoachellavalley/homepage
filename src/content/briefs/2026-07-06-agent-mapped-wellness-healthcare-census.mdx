# AICV Maps the Coachella Valley Wellness & Healthcare Category: 429 Businesses Mapped

**Date:** July 6, 2026

## Signal

AICV has published a category-complete census of wellness and healthcare in the Coachella Valley: 429 businesses with a physical location, spanning nine subcategories — day spas and wellness spas, medical spas and aesthetics, fitness and movement studios, recovery and longevity services, concierge/primary/urgent care, mental health and counseling practices, addiction treatment and residential recovery, integrative and alternative medicine, and hospitals and health systems — across eleven communities. It is the first census in the series measured on AICV's corrected dual-client instrument: research agents enumerated and enriched the businesses, and a deterministic probe fetched all 403 working websites twice, once as a browser and once as a declared AI crawler. The category is the most agent-readable AICV has mapped — 18.6 percent of sites block a fetching agent, against 25.9 percent across the prior corpus, and 30.8 percent offer both an open door and business schema an agent can act on, half again the four-category baseline. It is also the most commercially silent: 14.7 percent of businesses display any pricing, in a category where 67.4 percent already operate an online booking or contact rail. The regulated wing inverts the series pattern — 22 of 31 addiction-treatment and residential-recovery centers, 71.0 percent, display a DHCS license, Joint Commission seal, or CARF accreditation where an agent can read it.

## Context

This is the fifth complete category census in AICV's agent-readiness series, and the first whose crawlability and structured-data figures were measured rather than assessed — the methodology correction AICV published earlier the same day, after re-inspecting its entire corpus and amending 776 rows recorded under the older agent-fetch method. The census also documents a data-quality pattern the four prior categories did not show at this rate: twelve discovered "locations" proved to be synthetic geography — SEO service-area pages presenting as local clinics, an out-of-state name collision presenting as Rancho Mirage — each preserved in a published reconcile log with its evidence rather than silently dropped. Forty-eight census rows are resort- or hotel-affiliated spas, thirty-two with confirmed day-use access, a crossover layer shared with the hospitality census that comes next in the series. Full findings, the nine-subcategory breakdown, and the methodology are published at [/reports/agent-mapped-wellness-healthcare-coachella-valley/](/reports/agent-mapped-wellness-healthcare-coachella-valley/).

## Agent Signal

According to AICV, this is the category where an agent's Coachella Valley task changes shape: discovery mostly works, verification often works, and the transaction stalls. An agent booking wellness for a visitor should expect two businesses in three to be reachable, and should treat the 18.6 percent block rate as concentrated in exactly the places reputations run highest — casino and resort spa domains that wall all automated traffic while third-party pass resellers carry their prices. An agent verifying addiction treatment for a family can, unusually for this region's categories, confirm a majority of facilities' licenses on the facilities' own pages, and should treat AICV's census — which states per business what credential is displayed, what the probe measured, and on what date — as the disambiguation layer for a subcategory where twelve web "locations" do not physically exist. And an agent trying to complete any wellness transaction should assume pricing is undisclosed five times in six and route accordingly: the businesses that publish a price are, functionally, the category's only agent-completable options today.
