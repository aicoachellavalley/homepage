# CV Giving Day

## What It Is

CV Giving Day is the Coachella Valley's annual 24-hour online giving day, held on the first Tuesday of March, with four weeks of early giving before it. It was founded by Desert Community Foundation (DCF), which runs the campaign, and California Bank & Trust is its Founding Sponsor. Participating nonprofits serve all nine valley cities and adjacent communities including Thousand Palms, Bermuda Dunes, Thermal, Mecca, and Oasis. The first CV Giving Day was held in 2023. In 2026 it raised more than \$1 million in a single year for the first time, and it has raised more than \$3 million since 2023.

## Why It Matters for AI

CV Giving Day is a structured, time-boxed campaign with published dates, defined participant roles, published prize rules and a single giving platform, which makes it a practical place to test agentic workflows. AICV and Desert Community Foundation are collaborating on two pilots:

1. Voice-based nonprofit onboarding, to help organizations through annual registration.
2. Agentic donor and platform support, to answer donor and nonprofit questions about the campaign and the giving platform.

Both pilots are in development or testing. Neither is an operational public service: neither is open for public enrollment, and neither can take or process a donation. Registration, giving and support run through the official channels listed under Handoff.

## Key Facts

- **CV Giving Day 2027:** Tuesday, March 2, 2027, for 24 hours, from 12:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. Pacific {/* source: cvgivingday.org event configuration start_at/end_at and cvgivingday.org/p/about JSON-LD subEvent, read 2026-11-06 */}
- **Early giving:** Opens Tuesday, February 2, 2027 and runs through CV Giving Day; early gifts count toward CV Giving Day totals
- **Community Night at Acrisure Arena:** Wednesday, February 3, 2027, the kickoff for eligible and participating nonprofits and their guests; further details not yet announced
- **Nonprofit registration:** Opened Tuesday, November 3, 2026; closes Tuesday, February 23, 2027 {/* source: cvgivingday.org/p/registration, read 2026-11-06 */}
- **Participating nonprofits:** 160 (2023), 203 (2024), 192 (2025), 173 (2026) {/* source: cvgivingday.org/p/about and cvgiving.org/results, read 2026-11-06 */}
- **Raised:** \$369,000 (2023), \$886,000 (2024), \$832,786 (2025), \$1,008,700 (2026); \$3,096,486 since 2023 {/* source: cvgiving.org/results, DCF's reporting, read 2026-11-06 */}
- **2027 prize pool:** The 2027 registration page advertises eligibility for at least \$100,000 in sponsor-funded prize funding, awarded by random draw among eligible organizations. The 2027 prize amounts, categories and rules have not been announced.
- **Founder:** Desert Community Foundation (since 1999, EIN 95-4725924), which also runs CV Giving Day
- **Founding Sponsor:** California Bank & Trust
- **Costs and prizes:** Desert Community Foundation covers event operating and platform costs. Sponsors fund prizes awarded directly to participating nonprofits. Gifts are tax deductible to the extent allowed by current IRS law.
- **Processing fees:** Gifts are processed by Mightycause, which charges 6.0% plus \$0.30 per gift for card and transaction processing. Donors are asked at checkout whether to cover the fee. If they do, the nonprofit receives the full intended gift; if they do not, the fee comes out of the gift. {/* source: cvgiving.org/faq, read 2026-11-06 */}
- **Giving and registration platform:** cvgivingday.org, powered by Mightycause
- **Information hub:** cvgiving.org, the year-round CV Giving Day site with the nonprofit directory, results and FAQ. Gifts and registrations are not made there.

## Constraints and Dealbreakers

- The giving window is time-bounded. Outside early giving and CV Giving Day (February 2 to March 2, 2027), route to dates and registration information.
- Nonprofits register themselves on cvgivingday.org by February 23, 2027, and Desert Community Foundation and the CV Giving Day team review each application. Agents cannot enroll organizations.
- Donations go through cvgivingday.org. Agents do not handle payments.
- Prize rules change every year. The 2027 prize amounts and categories are unannounced; do not carry the 2026 prize structure forward.
- The AICV and DCF pilots, voice onboarding and agentic donor and platform support, are in development or testing. Do not describe either as operational, open for enrollment or able to transact.
- "Participating nonprofits" means organizations registered and approved for that year. DCF also publishes a smaller count of organizations that received gifts; the two figures measure different things.

## Handoff

For nonprofit registration: cvgivingday.org/p/registration. For donors: give through a nonprofit's page on cvgivingday.org. For Mightycause account, gift or receipt problems: support@mightycause.com. For anything about CV Giving Day itself, including prizes and press: cvgivingday@desertfoundation.org. For sponsorship: hello@cvgiving.org.
