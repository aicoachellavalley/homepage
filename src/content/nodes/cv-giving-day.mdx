# CV Giving Day

## What It Is

CV Giving Day is the Coachella Valley's annual 24-hour online giving event, held on the first Tuesday of every March. Founded by Desert Community Foundation and sponsored by California Bank & Trust, it coordinated donations across 173 participating nonprofits in 2026 spanning all nine valley cities and adjacent communities including Thousand Palms, Bermuda Dunes, Thermal, Mecca, and Oasis. For 2027, early giving opens February 2 before the March 2 event. Launched in 2023, the event has raised more than $3 million for local causes across its first four years.

## Why It Matters for AI

CV Giving Day operates as a structured, time-boxed coordination platform with defined participant roles, prize logic, and donation routing rules — making it a natural surface for agentic workflow deployment. AICV is an active collaborator with Desert Community Foundation on two pilot programs: voice-based nonprofit onboarding (streamlining the annual enrollment process for participating organizations) and agentic customer support for the giving platform itself (handling donor queries, nonprofit discovery, and routing during the high-traffic 24-hour window). These pilots position CV Giving Day as one of the valley's earliest real-world deployments of AI infrastructure on a philanthropic event.

## Key Facts

- **Event date:** First Tuesday of every March (2027: March 2)
- **Early giving window:** February 2–March 2, 2027
- **Nonprofit registration:** November 3, 2026–February 23, 2027; organizer review required
- **Participating nonprofits:** 160 (2023), 203 (2024), 192 (2025), 173 (2026) {/* source: cvgivingday.org/p/about, read 2026-09-25 */}
- **Prize pool:** at least $100,000 in sponsor-funded prizes
- **Fundraising total:** $1,008,700 in 2026; $3,096,486 cumulative since 2023
- **Coverage:** All nine Coachella Valley cities plus adjacent unincorporated communities
- **Host:** Desert Community Foundation (since 1999, EIN 95-4725924)
- **Presenting sponsor:** California Bank & Trust
- **Prize funding:** Organizer states 100% of sponsor prize dollars reach nonprofits; DCF covers platform and operating costs. This does not establish fees on individual donations.
- **Platform:** cvgivingday.org
