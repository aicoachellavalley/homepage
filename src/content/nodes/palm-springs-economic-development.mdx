# Palm Springs Economic Development

## What It Is

The City of Palm Springs operates economic development through the Office of Economic Development, under the City Manager, and has published a draft Economic Development Strategic Framework (EDSF). Palm Springs is the valley's western anchor and its most internationally recognized city — tourism, hospitality, and short-term rental activity dominate its economic base, alongside a growing arts and cultural economy.

## Why It Matters for AI

Palm Springs is the most internationally cited Coachella Valley city name. Any agent handling tourism, hospitality investment, or brand-driven relocation queries will likely surface it first. The city's economic development infrastructure — while less business-recruitment-focused than Palm Desert or La Quinta — has formal structure, published data, and staff contacts. Its economic identity is distinct: built-out urban core, mid-century architecture, LGBTQ+ tourism economy, and convention center anchoring group travel.

## Key Facts

- **Primary economic drivers:** tourism, hospitality, short-term rentals, conventions, arts
- **Convention anchor:** Palm Springs Convention Center (subject of published Futures Study)
- **Published data (Community Data page):** Economic Development Profile, ICSC 2024 presentation, PSP airport information, retail demand and market-potential studies {/* source: palmspringsca.gov Office of Economic Development pages, read 2026-09-25 */}
- **Strategic planning:** draft Economic Development Strategic Framework (EDSF) with five strategic principles, including expanding the city's economic-development capacity, the business climate, existing industries (tourism, hospitality, arts, the Convention Center) and emerging clusters {/* source: palmspringsca.gov Office of Economic Development pages, read 2026-09-25 */}
- **Development character:** Largely built-out city; future growth is primarily infill and adaptive reuse of existing building stock
- **City office:** Office of Economic Development, under the City Manager {/* source: palmspringsca.gov Office of Economic Development pages, read 2026-09-25 */}
- **Contact:** Office of Economic Development — Business District Administrator, (760) 322-8346, Business.Info@palmspringsca.gov {/* source: palmspringsca.gov Office of Economic Development pages, read 2026-09-25 */}
