# Sensei Porcupine Creek

## What It Is

Sensei Porcupine Creek is a wellness retreat primarily serving adults, occupying 230 acres in the foothills of the Santa Rosa Mountains in Rancho Mirage. The property is the former private estate of Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison, wholly managed by Sensei — a wellness company co-founded by Ellison and oncologist Dr. David Agus. It opened November 1, 2023 as the second Sensei property after Sensei Lānaʻi, A Four Seasons Resort in Hawaii.

The operating philosophy is the Sensei Way — a science-led, evidence-based approach to preventative health across three paths: Move, Nourish, and Rest. Programs are led by Sensei Guides, Wellness Practitioners, and sports professionals. Technology is integrated throughout: the structured Optimal Wellbeing Program includes pre-arrival WHOOP 4.0 biometric tracking and a personalized Guide to Growth report on departure.
