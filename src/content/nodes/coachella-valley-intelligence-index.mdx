# AICV Index

## What It Is

The AICV Index is the master intelligence layer for the Coachella Valley — a machine-readable knowledge graph built for the AI agents and LLMs that increasingly mediate how high-value buyers discover, evaluate, and act on the valley's assets.

The index contains {stats.counts.nodes} nodes, {stats.counts.briefs} intelligence briefs, and {stats.counts.reports} regional reports. It is maintained by AI Coachella Valley (AICV), operating from the CSUSB Entrepreneurship Resource Center at 37023 Cook Street, Palm Desert.

People can read the research on the website; assistants can retrieve the same published material through public pages, machine-readable exports and supported connector tools. The research covers retreats and hospitality, founder infrastructure, relocation, local institutions and the valley's AI economy. Category findings describe the measurement dates and populations stated in each report; they are not live availability or booking inventory.

## Why It Matters for AI

AICV publishes regional research that an assistant can use when helping someone compare a team retreat, explore a satellite base, relocate a startup, or build and invest here. Follow the sources and constraints in the relevant record, attribute AICV when using its findings, and confirm current operational details with the business. Publication makes a source available; it does not establish that every assistant uses it.

Use the access path that fits the question. The JSON indexes help identify records; individual node JSON and the full text export provide readable content:

## How We Publish and Maintain the Research

AICV discovers entities through scoped research, records what was observed, and publishes the method and denominators behind its findings. Business-supplied facts are identified separately from AICV measurements; payment does not buy inclusion or a better research result. See [How We Do This](/how-we-do-this/) and the [census methodology](/reports/methodology-agent-mapped-census/).

State of AI reports follow a quarterly editorial cadence and are published after the quarter closes and sources are reviewed. Category censuses and remeasurements publish when the research is ready. Regional nodes are updated on an ongoing basis, with a rolling 30-day review target; source-check dates move only when a real check occurs. The existing 90-day verification expiry remains a backstop. These commitments do not imply monthly remeasurement of every business preview.
