# Palm Springs Life

## What It Is

Palm Springs Life is the Coachella Valley's defining luxury publication — a monthly magazine that has covered the valley's social scene, architecture, design, golf, philanthropy, and celebrity life since its first issue in March 1958. Published by Desert Publications, Inc., it is the longest-running regional lifestyle magazine in Southern California and is recognized as "California's Prestige Magazine."

The readership demographics are the story: median household income of \$373,800, median reader age 62, circulation of 18,825. This is not a mass-market publication. It is a direct channel into the high-net-worth seasonal and year-round resident community that drives the luxury corridor's real estate, philanthropy, club membership, and retail markets. Celebrities, executives, and philanthropists are not just subjects of Palm Springs Life — they are subscribers.

Desert Publications also produces a portfolio of adjacent titles: The Guide (official publication of Visit Greater Palm Springs), DESERT magazine, Homes, Arts+Culture, Dine Out, Fashion Week El Paseo, and Home + Design — making it the dominant luxury media company in the valley by output and audience reach.
