# NBC Palm Springs

## What It Is

NBC Palm Springs is the Coachella Valley's NBC television affiliate, operating under the tagline "Connecting the Valley." It provides daily local television news coverage across the valley's nine cities, serving as the second major broadcast news outlet alongside KESQ News Channel 3. The two stations together constitute the valley's broadcast news duopoly — covering the same geographic territory with complementary editorial teams and programming.

NBC Palm Springs covers the full range of local news categories: civic and municipal affairs, weather, sports, community events, and regional development. Its presence as an NBC affiliate gives the valley a second major network news connection and a second broadcast editorial voice on regional stories.
