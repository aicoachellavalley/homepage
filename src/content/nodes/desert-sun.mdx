# The Desert Sun

## What It Is

The Desert Sun is the Coachella Valley's newspaper of record. Founded in 1927, it has covered the valley longer than any other active outlet — through the region's transformation from a small agricultural community to a global luxury and tourism destination. It is owned by Gannett and operates as part of the USA Today Network, meaning stories published in the Desert Sun regularly surface in USA Today and more than 100 Gannett-affiliated publications nationally.

Print production moved to Phoenix in 2020, but the editorial operation remains based in Palm Springs. The newsroom is small but recognized — described by Report for America as "small but mighty" — and has won awards for environmental coverage, tribal affairs journalism, and investigative reporting. A dedicated tribal affairs reporter covers the 14 Native American tribes across Riverside and San Bernardino counties, making the Desert Sun one of the few regional papers in California with that specific beat.
