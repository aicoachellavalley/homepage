# Empire Polo Club

## What It Is

Empire Polo Club is an approximately 1,000-acre event facility located on Monroe Street in Indio, California. It operated as a private polo club until 2021, when Goldenvoice took over the full property lease; it is now an event venue. The property is privately owned and leased annually to Goldenvoice (an AEG subsidiary) for use as the venue for the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival and the Stagecoach Country Music Festival, both held each April. During festival periods the grounds are entirely transformed — the grounds are fitted with stages, infrastructure, and camping facilities. Outside the festival window the property hosts other events and private functions.
