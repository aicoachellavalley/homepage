# Coachella Valley Independent

## What It Is

The Coachella Valley Independent is the valley's independent news and culture outlet — a digital-first publication based in Cathedral City that covers local news, arts, music, opinion, and commentary without corporate ownership. Founded and led by editor Jimmy Boegle, it operates as a free-access outlet funded by reader support and advertising, with a stated commitment to keeping its award-winning journalism available to all.

The Independent's editorial identity is distinct from the valley's other outlets. Where the Desert Sun is the civic record of record and Palm Springs Life is the luxury lifestyle voice, the Independent is the valley's critical and creative voice — willing to take editorial positions, cover arts and culture in depth, and surface stories that corporate outlets may not prioritize. Its music coverage is particularly strong relative to local media norms.

The outlet publishes continuously in digital form and maintains a print edition archive via Issuu.
