Hotel Paseo's Palm Boardroom **does not fit 16-person strategy work**: 16 is reception, conference is 14 and U-shape is 12. Its Paseo A/B sections publish conference 38. Fairfield Room fits 16 in conference 18 only; classroom/U-shape are 15. Hyatt Primrose boardroom is 14; Hibiscus A/B and Lupine/Desertbell boardroom are 18. Ritz Boardroom conference 20 fits 16, but its U-shape 12 does not; Morningside publishes U-shape 18.

Parker's [official member chart](https://www.lhw.com/hotel/Parker-Palm-Springs-CA-USA/event-venues) establishes Murphy Room classroom 33/theater 40, not conference/boardroom. Its duplicated Ballroom A labels and zeros are uncertainty, not missing capacity to infer. Sensei and Two Bunch also remain inquiry-only for 16-person strategy working capacity. Sensei's 48-person buyout maximum is not meeting-room capacity; Two Bunch Glass House gathering limit is 15, while View Point has a corporate meeting pathway without a layout number.

Sensei [groups](https://sensei.com/retreats/porcupine-creek/group-inquiries/) require buyout at 10+ rooms. [FAQ](https://sensei.com/faq/) requires two nights and ordinary age 18+, or 16+ with an adult; children of all ages are permitted during estate buyouts. Shared-room arrangements cannot be assumed to avoid buyout. Two Bunch [FAQ](https://twobunchpalms.com/faq/) requires 18+; Lawn/Courtyard is partial/full-buyout-only. Its limited [individual day access](https://twobunchpalms.com/offers/) does not establish group meeting rental.

Hotel “practical” labels are editorial service-format judgments, not verified lower costs. No hotel has an all-in November retreat quote. Courtyard Palm Springs [publishes](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/pspch-courtyard-palm-springs/events/) coffee break $12/person and lunch $25/person, component prices only. Lodging, rental, AV, catering minimums, taxes and service fees remain unresolved.

Official lodging evidence includes Paseo 150 rooms/suites, Sensei 22 accommodations, Two Bunch 65 rooms with 16 Double Rooms, Ace Standard Double with two double beds, and Residence Inn suites with full kitchens/separate spaces/sofa beds. None establishes a currently available shared-room block.

ARRIVE is candidate-only to avoid padding: [official page](https://www.arrivehotels.com/hotels/palm-springs/meetings-and-events) establishes 32 rooms, two-queen options, adults-only policy and company retreats. The boardroom/conference room is 12; convertible classroom lounge capacity is unspecified.

Unknowns: date-specific room/bed mix, price, AV, catering, accessible room inventory and routes, acoustic privacy and concurrent events. Field classifications, named layouts, official source URLs and restrictions are in `retreat-venues-2026-10-07.json`. Only these two research files were edited.
